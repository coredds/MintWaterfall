Profit & loss bridge

Increases and decreases are colored automatically. Gross profit and Operating income are subtotals; Net income is the grand total. Hover, click, or tab through the bars.

Click a bar to inspect it.
const chart = MintWaterfall.waterfallChart()
  .showTotal(true).totalLabel("Net income")
  .formatNumber(d3.format("$,.0f"))
  .enableTooltips(true).responsive(true)
  .on("barClick", (event, d) => console.log(d.label, d.barTotal));

d3.select("#chart").datum([
  { label: "Revenue", stacks: [{ value: 5200 }] },
  { label: "Cost of sales", stacks: [{ value: -2100 }] },
  { label: "Gross profit", subtotal: true },  // running total so far
  ...
]).call(chart);

Stacked segments

Each step can be broken into labeled segments, with a legend. Q1 opening is an opening balance (start: true): drawn like a total, not as an increase.

Themes

Built-in themes restyle bars, grid, axes and text. Auto follows the reader's light/dark setting.

Trend lines

Overlay a linear, polynomial or moving-average fit of the running total.

Brush, zoom & export

Drag to select bars, scroll to zoom, Shift+drag to pan. Export the current chart as SVG, PNG or CSV.

Drag across the chart to select a range.

Statistics

The statistics module summarises a series and fits a trend — useful alongside the chart.
